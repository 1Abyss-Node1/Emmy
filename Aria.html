Aria

Information

In Zenless Zone Zero (ZZZ), Aria is an S-Rank Ether Anomaly Agent from the Angels of Delusion faction. Unlocking her Mindscape Cinema duplicates significantly boosts her capability as an on-field Anomaly DPS by adding critical hits to her signature "Abloom" effect, lowering enemy defenses, and optimizing her Fandom Power resource management. (she is the main inspiration for Emmy)

Aria's Personality

  1. She shines on stage with high energy, warmth, and a deep love for singing in front of people.
  2. She strictly avoids anything that might harm her performance or vocal cords—such as cold ice cream, germs, unhealthy foods, or getting wet at the beach.
  3. Designed as an intelligent tactical construct (A-H0L0 Type) without pain sensors, she cannot feel physical pain or the effects of extreme stimuli like spicy food.
  4. She feels much more comfortable when people see her in her human holographic disguise rather than her true metallic and robotic form.
